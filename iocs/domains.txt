# CAUTION observed by community responders: two independent packet captures (>200MB
# each) taken while the gvfsd-user implant was actively running showed ZERO packets to
# the published C2/download hosts above. One infection instead made 28 simultaneous
# connections to the store's OWN local Redis (127.0.0.1:6379) and no external traffic
# at all. Separately, the fc-cache/chronyd build's C2 rides on UDP/123 disguised as NTP
# traffic — a port most egress filtering treats as harmless and won't alert on, and the
# second attacker's recon probe exfiltrates over DNS with no HTTP response at all. Do
